Good to know

Wedding Photography questions

Can’t find your answer? WhatsApp Sandip. We reply within 24 hours.

How much does a candid wedding photographer cost in Mumbai?

Our wedding collections start at ₹35,000 per day for the Essential collection: 6 hours of coverage, one candid and one traditional photographer, 150–200 edited photos and an online gallery. Silver starts at ₹55,000, Gold at ₹85,000 and Platinum at ₹1,35,000. Your final quote depends on dates, hours and venue.

Do you shoot candid or traditional wedding photos?

Both. Every collection includes a candid photographer for natural, emotional moments and a traditional photographer for rituals, stage and family group photos.

How many photos will we receive?

It depends on the collection: 150–200 edited photos in Essential, 250–300 in Silver, 400–500 in Gold and 600–700 in Platinum, plus a complete raw image backup.

Do you travel outside Mumbai for weddings?

Yes. We regularly cover Thane, Navi Mumbai and Pune, and travel across India and abroad for destination weddings. Outstation travel and stay are charged separately.

WhatsApp +91 70452 78377 with your date and city. We confirm availability within 24 hours, send a detailed quotation, and your date is reserved once the booking advance is received.
