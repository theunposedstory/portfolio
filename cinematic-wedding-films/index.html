Cinematic Films questions

Can’t find your answer? WhatsApp Sandip. We reply within 24 hours.

What is the difference between a teaser, a highlight film and a full video?

The teaser is a 1–3 minute trailer for social media. The highlight film is a 10–25 minute cinematic story of your day. The full event video is 1–3 hours of traditional documentation of every ritual and speech.

Which collections include a wedding film?

Silver includes a highlights reel. Gold and Platinum include a full cinematic wedding film, and Gold also includes drone coverage. Reels can be added to any collection.

How much does wedding videography cost in Mumbai?

Photo + video collections start at ₹55,000 per day (Silver: 2 photographers + 1 videographer). Gold with two cinematographers and a cinematic film starts at ₹85,000 per day.

Do you offer drone shots?

Yes. Drone aerial coverage is included in Gold and can be added to other collections, subject to venue permissions.

How will we receive our films?

Final films are delivered through a Google Drive link and on a USB pen drive.
