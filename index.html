Questions couples ask us

Can’t find your answer? WhatsApp Sandip — we reply within 24 hours.

How much does a wedding photographer cost in Mumbai with The Unposed Story?

Wedding collections start at ₹35,000 per day for intimate ceremonies — 6 hours of coverage, a candid and a traditional photographer, 150–200 edited photos and an online gallery. Larger collections add cinematic films, drone coverage, premium albums and a pre-wedding shoot. Your final quote depends on dates, hours and location.

Do you cover destination weddings?

Yes. We’re based in Mumbai, photograph weddings across India, and travel internationally for destination weddings.

What is included in every package?

Every collection includes professional editing and online gallery delivery. Higher collections add highlight reels or cinematic wedding films, drone aerial coverage, and 30- or 40-page premium albums.

Do you offer pre-wedding shoots in Mumbai?

Yes — at beaches, heritage forts and golden-hour locations across Mumbai and beyond. A pre-wedding shoot is included in Platinum and can also be booked on its own.
